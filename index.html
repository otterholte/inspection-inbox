Getting a memo from Voice Memos (iPhone)

iPhone keeps Voice Memos inside its own app, so they don't show up in Files until you save a copy:

  1. Open Voice Memos and tap the recording.
  2. Tap ••• → Save to Files → On My iPhone (or iCloud Drive) → Save.
  3. Come back here, tap Choose memo file → Browse → open the same place and tap the memo.

Faster: tap Record memo above and record right in this app. You can pause and cancel.
